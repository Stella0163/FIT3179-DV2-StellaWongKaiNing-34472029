Part 7: Egg farms by size

Where are Australia's small, medium and large egg farms?

These three maps compare the geographical distribution of Australia's 1,470 egg-laying farms in 2024. Each map shows one farm-size category, making differences in concentration easier to see.

Small farms (1,193 nationwide)

How to use this mapHover over a state for its small-farm count and share of the national small-farm total.

Small farms

Victoria accounts for 789 of the country's 1,193 small egg farms, substantially more than any other state.

Medium farms (143 nationwide)

How to use this mapHover over a state for its medium-farm count and share of the national medium-farm total.

Medium farms

Victoria has 63 medium farms, followed by New South Wales with 43 and Queensland with 21.

Large farms (134 nationwide)

How to use this mapHover over a state for its large-farm count and share of the national large-farm total.

Large farms

Victoria has 63 large farms and New South Wales has 39, together accounting for most large farms.

Note: The maps show farm counts, not numbers of laying hens or egg-production volumes. Each percentage uses the total for that farm-size category.

Part 9: Egg consumption

Egg consumption shows changes in Australian demand

Annual consumption per person provides a demand-side perspective alongside Australia's egg production figures.

How to use this chartHover over each bar to view the actual egg consumption and its difference from the four-year average. Blue represents above-average consumption, while orange represents below-average consumption.

Demand over time

Egg consumption exceeded the four-year average in 2022–23 and 2023–24, before declining below average in 2024–25. This highlights recent changes in egg consumption patterns across Australia.

Part 10: Restaurant egg meal prices

Prepared egg meal prices vary between capital cities

An April 2026 map compares the median listed café price of an “Eggs Your Way” meal across six Australian capitals.

How to use this mapEach circle marks a capital city. Darker blue indicates a higher median price; hover over a marker for the exact price in Australian dollars.

Differences across cities

Perth has the highest median meal price in this dataset ($16.00), while Sydney has the lowest ($14.00). These are prepared café meal prices, not farm-gate egg prices, so they provide market context rather than a direct measure of production.
