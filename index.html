Australia's poultry industry: from growth to geography

This visual story explores how Australia's chicken meat and egg

industries have changed over time, where production is concentrated,

and how the industry's economic significance is developing.

Part 1: Long-term growth

Chicken meat production has expanded strongly over time

Production has generally increased over the decades, although some years show temporary slowdowns or declines.

How to use this chart: hover over the chart to read exact values for each year.

What this shows

The chart highlights both overall production growth and year-to-year variation, helping separate the long-term direction from short-term fluctuations.

Part 2: Recent production

Recent production remains high

Recent quarterly data give a closer look at chicken meat output, slaughter numbers and industry value.

How to use this chart: Each panel shows percentage change relative to June 2024. Blue indicates growth and orange indicates decline. Darker shades represent changes beyond the first 6 percentage points. Hover over the areas to view actual values and percentage changes.

Recent movement

This chart focuses on shorter-term changes, showing whether recent production continues the broader long-term growth story.

Part 3: Growth in volume and value

Industry growth is visible in both production and economic value

Physical output and monetary value capture two different dimensions of industry growth.

How to use this chart: hover over each bar to compare values between the two reported periods.

Production volume

Compares the amount of chicken meat produced between the two reported periods.

Gross production value

Shows how the dollar value of chicken production changed over the same period.

Part 4: Geography

Chicken production is concentrated in major producing states

State-level data reveal where a large share of Australia's chicken meat production is concentrated.

How to use this chart: hover over a state to see its production share.

State distribution

New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland are reported individually, while the remaining states are grouped together in the source data.

Part 5: Egg production

Egg production is concentrated across several major states

Comparing state shares shows how national egg production is distributed across Australia's major producing regions.

How to use these charts: hover over a slice or bar to see the exact percentage for that state.

Overall distribution

The first chart shows how national egg production is divided between the main producing states and the combined remainder.

Exact comparison

The bar chart makes the percentage differences easier to compare precisely across states.

Part 6: Laying flock

The laying flock shows where egg production capacity is located

The distribution of laying hens shows where the productive capacity of Australia's egg industry sits.

How to use this chart: hover over a state to see its share of the national laying flock.

Different measure, different story

This chart shows each state's share of the national laying flock, so it should not be read as direct egg output.

Part 7: Industry value

Poultry and egg production make a substantial economic contribution

Production value provides another perspective on the scale of Australia's poultry industry, comparing the economic value generated by poultry slaughtering and egg production.

How to use this chart: hover over each point to see the production value and financial year.

Economic scale

Poultry slaughtering has a higher production value than egg production, showing the relative economic scale of the two parts of Australia's poultry industry.

Part 8: Future outlook

Forecasts point to continued expansion

Historical growth explains how the industry reached its current position. Forecasts show where production and exports may move next.

How to use this chart: hover over a forecast value to see the exact figure for that year.

Looking forward

Forecast values extend the story beyond historical production and show the industry's expected direction.

A poultry industry shaped by long-term growth

Together, these visualisations show sustained growth in chicken meat production, strong geographic concentration in both chicken and egg production, and continuing economic importance across Australia's poultry industry.
